# RoutineNest privacy policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

RoutineNest is published by Lanray. This policy describes the current RoutineNest Android app, version 0.2.0, which provides local routine planning, checklists, reminders and optional speech features. Premium purchasing is disabled in this version.

## Routine data on your device

RoutineNest stores routine and task names, schedules, completion history, reminder settings and appearance/language preferences on your device. These records support your checklists, history, reminders and progress views. RoutineNest does not upload this routine database to a developer server. Core routine features do not require a RoutineNest account.

You choose the content you enter. Avoid entering sensitive personal information that you do not want stored on your device. RoutineNest contains no advertising or developer analytics tracker.

## Optional speech features

Voice input uses your device's installed speech-recognition service after you grant microphone permission and start recognition. That service may send audio and recognized text to its provider for processing under the provider's privacy policy. RoutineNest uses the returned text to carry out supported routine commands and does not keep an audio recording in its routine database. Typed commands are available without microphone access. RoutineNest does not continuously listen in the background.

Optional spoken responses use your installed text-to-speech engine. That engine may process supplied text on the device or through its provider. You can use RoutineNest without voice input or spoken responses and revoke microphone permission in device settings.

## Amazon services and purchasing

The app includes the Amazon Appstore SDK for product and account availability. The SDK exchanges information with Amazon and can return an Amazon account identifier, marketplace and product information to the app. Service connections can expose network/device identifiers, such as an IP address, to the provider. Amazon's services process data under Amazon's own privacy terms. RoutineNest does not receive your payment card details.

Purchasing and receipt verification remain disabled in this version. The app does not currently create a Supabase billing account or send receipts to its configured verification service. Any future release enabling billing accounts or receipt verification will update this policy before those features are enabled.

## Reminders and exports

Reminders use local device scheduling and notifications, subject to permissions and operating-system limits. RoutineNest does not send reminder contents to a developer notification server.

If you export your routine data, the app writes it to the destination you select. A cloud storage or sharing provider you choose may process that file under its own terms. Exported copies remain under your control and are not removed by the app's local deletion action.

## Data deletion

To delete local routines and completion history, open RoutineNest, go to Settings, choose Delete routine data and confirm. The app removes those records and cancels their scheduled reminders. Appearance/language preferences are separate. You can remove all local app storage, including preferences and any locally cached SDK information, using your device's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Delete exported files separately from the destinations where you saved them.

RoutineNest cannot delete information held independently by Amazon or your chosen speech, storage or sharing provider. Use the relevant provider's account/privacy controls for those requests. There is no RoutineNest account to delete in the current version.

## Retention and security

Local routine records remain until you delete them or remove app storage. Android app backups are disabled by the app manifest, although device/vendor transfer tools may have their own behavior. Protect your device and exported files using your device's security controls. Data processed by third-party providers follows their retention and security policies.

## Intended audience

The current release is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact the publisher at **banksmi@mail.com**, the support email supplied in RoutineNest's Amazon listing. You may also contact the publisher through the [RoutineNest GitHub repository](https://github.com/lanray07/RoutineNest/issues). GitHub issues are public: do not post account identifiers, receipts, private routine data or other sensitive information there. Information you voluntarily send for support is used to respond to your request; the email or GitHub provider also processes it under its own terms.

Updates to this policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. The policy and store disclosures must be updated when the app's data practices change.
